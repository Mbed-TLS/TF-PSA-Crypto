Bugfix
   * Fix psa_hash_update(), psa_hash_compute() and psa_hash_compare()
     computing the wrong digest (or comparison result) for PSA_ALG_MD5,
     PSA_ALG_RIPEMD160, PSA_ALG_SHA_1, PSA_ALG_SHA_224 and
     PSA_ALG_SHA_256 when a single call is passed 4GiB or more of
     input on a 64-bit platform. Reported by TimothyPMann in
     Mbed-TLS/mbedtls#10964.
