Changes
   * AES-GCM size and performance improvements. Performance on Arm Neon
     benefits around 2.4x - 3.6x; scalar improves by 3% - 10%. Size is reduced
     by up to 700 bytes.
     If MBEDTLS_AESCE_OPTIMISE_FOR_SIZE is set, Arm Neon performance increase
     is reduced (up to 35% improvement), but size is reduced
     by around 750 bytes (clang) or 1400 bytes (gcc).
